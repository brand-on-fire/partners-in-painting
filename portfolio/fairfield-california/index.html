Project Description

Fairfield, California

While Partners in Painting specializes in ‘classic’ painting projects in the Napa Valley region of California, that is not all we do. We are also available for ‘standard’ residential painting projects of smaller properties and track homes. No project is too small or too large for Partners in Painting.

This project in Fairfield, California was completed in October 2014. The property was painted in a rich Hampstead green with white trim. The doors were painted in a darker shade of the same color, thus providing the visual contrast seen in the photos above. A special gray was used for the foundation to accent the main house color.

Partners in Painting

Mike Gibson, of Partners in Painting, has painted hundreds of homes throughout the Napa Valley. The projects that come with history bring him the most joy. He learned how to faux paint and work with wood finishes. Mike is an expert at color matching and working with the more decorative details that come with older homes. Read More >
